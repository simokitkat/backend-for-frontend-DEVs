# Module 5. History and storing results

![Badge for the History and storing results module](../../../public/a1-module-badges/05-history-storage.svg)

Finish the transformation pipeline: save the results, show the history to the owner, and serve files safely, taking the retention period into account.

## Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

- design the metadata and paginated reading of the history;
- save files through a separate storage adapter;
- limit downloads to the owner and to an administrator's permissions;
- account for retries, partial failures, the audit trail, and the retention period.
